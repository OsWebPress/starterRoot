# Sailing

I started sailing when I was six and never really stopped. Over the years I have sailed on a lot of different boats, from an optimist to a 45ft yacht, which is about as big as things have gotten for me so far. I will pretty much sail anything that moves. One type I still want to try is a foiling dinghy.

I have also taught sailing across quite a range: optimist, laser, laser pico, keelboat (valk, flytour, randmeer), windsurf, youth catamaran, and lelyvlets at scouting. Each boat teaches you something different, which is part of what keeps it interesting.

This is not a full log of every sail, just the trips, races or thoughts that felt worth writing down.
