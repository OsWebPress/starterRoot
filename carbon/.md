# Hi, I'm Oswin
 
I enjoy creating things, and that's what got me into coding. I work in software engineering and outside of work I spend my time on the water or on a board of some kind.

I grew up in Curaçao, where I started sailing at the YSCO sailing school and later raced at WSV Jan Sofat, optimists first, then on a small yacht where we even sailed to Bonaire for a regatta once. After finishing VWO at the Peter Stuyvesant College I moved to the Netherlands, like most of my peers did. I studied at [Codam Coding College](https://codam.nl) in Amsterdam, a peer-to-peer school where you learn entirely by building things, which suited me well.

On the side I teach sailing at [Zeilschool Aalsmeer](https://zeilschoolaalsmeer.nl). I also windsurf, snowboard, and technically skateboard, though I'm not very good at that last one.
